Purpose

All the tools you need for
focused writing

Heavy word processors cost too much, and simple editors don’t feel complete enough to stay in flow. That’s why I built Word Pro.

The goal was a full-featured word processor that is completely free and open source, built around how you actually write: clear formatting, good structure, safe saving and easy sharing.

Because it’s open source, you can download the project and add, remove, or change any feature to fit your needs.

  • Writing & EditingOpen a clean page and start typing. Word Pro gives you rich text editing with undo and redo, cut, copy and paste, and find and replace, so you can rewrite freely without losing a thought. The page stays clear of clutter so the focus stays on your words.
  • Text FormattingChoose fonts and sizes, apply bold, italic and underline, colour your text, align it left, centre or right, and build bullet and numbered lists. The formatting buttons show the style of the text under your cursor, so you always know what is applied before you change it.
  • Styles & StructureHeadings, paragraph styles and quotes give every document a clear shape. Use headings for titles and sections, then move around long documents using the outline instead of scrolling for minutes. Good structure also makes your document easier to read, skim and share.
  • Page LayoutSet the page size and margins, add headers and footers, and number your pages. What you see on screen follows the page you will print or export, so the layout you design is the layout your readers get.
  • Tables & ImagesInsert tables for structured information and pictures for illustration. Select an image to move it, or drag a corner to resize it, and keep writing in the text around it. Tables and images stay where you put them when you save and reopen the document.
  • Saving Your WorkSave, Save As and Save a Copy cover every way you keep a document. Save updates the file you are working on, Save As lets you choose a new name and location, and Save a Copy keeps your original untouched. A clear unsaved-changes indicator shows when your latest edits are not stored yet, and Word Pro asks before closing a document so nothing is lost by accident. Recent documents bring your last files back in one click.
  • AutoSave & RecoveryAutoSave stores your work in the background at an interval you choose, so a long writing session never depends on remembering to press Save. If the app closes unexpectedly, Word Pro keeps a recovery copy and offers to restore it the next time you open it. Recovery copies are kept separate from your original file, so your saved version is never overwritten unless you decide to.
  • Export & SharingExport your finished document to PDF for sharing and printing, HTML for the web, or plain text for use anywhere. Each export keeps your headings, lists and emphasis, so what you send looks like what you wrote.
  • ProductivityA live word count, character count and reading time update as you type, and keyboard shortcuts cover the actions you use most. Zoom the page to suit your screen, and switch between light and dark mode for comfortable writing at any hour.
  • Free & Open SourceWord Pro is free and open source. Read the code, report problems, add the features you want or remove the ones you do not, and release your own build. It is built for Windows, macOS and Linux, and it is yours to keep and change.
